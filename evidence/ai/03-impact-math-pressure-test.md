# AI Debate Log 3 — Pressure-testing the impact math
Goal: a number that survives a live Q&A.

## The debate: lead with cost savings (floor) or platform revenue (ceiling)?
- Argument for ceiling-first: bigger number, excites investors.
- Attack: ceiling collapses by the SECOND question — "what's your developer/SMB adoption rate?" No real data supports it today.
- Resolution: FLOOR is primary (matches the brief's own template: cost ↓ → activity unlocked, no adoption assumption). CEILING is layer two, with a sensitivity table handed over before they ask.

## Grounding the inputs (sourced vs assumed)
- All-in cost 5% → DEFENSIBLE: FSB 2.7% avg / 6.1% sub-$500; Mastercard found a $250 payment can cost 23.3% (up to 30%); ~90% of non-US txns FX-converted without consent. → "5%" is conservative.
- Per-firm cross-border volume $1M → NO public figure exists (Mastercard tracked cost, not volume). Labeled: "[assumption — consistent with mid-sized SMB profile per Mastercard 2025]."
- Yield ~7% (Kira wallet); ~27M LatAm MSMEs; 3 of 5 pay international suppliers (only 13% export → payer base is the import side).

## The numbers
- Floor: ~$45K FX compression + ~$5K yield + ~$12.5K time = ~$62.5K/yr per SMB. Halve every input → still ~$31K. Corridor cross-check: 4.5% off a ~$1.37T-by-2030 corridor ≈ ~$60B/yr.
- Ceiling: 1% of 27M × ~$5K/account ≈ $1.35B/yr; sensitivity floor 0.1% ≈ $135M ≈ 45× current ~$3M ARR.

## Resolution
Two numbers, two audiences. Floor convinces the skeptic (no adoption assumption); ceiling excites the investor (assumption flagged + sensitivity table). Load-bearing inputs to defend: firm volume, idle balance, penetration.
