# AI Debate Log 1 — Candidate generation & kill-the-pet
Distilled from the working session. Goal: go from benchmark → 5 candidates → ONE, with named rejections.

## The 5 candidates (each tied to a benchmark takeaway)
1. Self-reconciling payouts — auto-match every payout to its ledger entry (Synapse $265M wound).
2. CFO-in-an-agent — agent runs the SMB cash cycle (copilot→operator takeaway).
3. Programmable conditional payouts — event-triggered escrow on stablecoin rails.
4. Compliance-as-code FX — KYB/AML/BCB-519-521 enforced at transaction time.
5. A2A payment accounts — bank the agent as a first-class account holder.

## Kill-the-pet (steelman → kill shot → verdict)

### 1. Self-reconciling payouts
- Steelman: best moat; Kira owns the full data chain; removes real ops cost.
- Kill shot: back-office win; no "billion people" story; un-Loomable.
- Verdict: survives weak. Great moat, no story.

### 2. CFO-in-an-agent
- Steelman: purest operator thesis; all primitives exist; most fundable.
- Kill shot: already shipped ("AI-Powered Treasury & Wallets" on kira.finance) → "isn't this your current product?" Boldness collapses.
- Verdict: survives weak.

### 3. Programmable conditional payouts
- Steelman: solves a real LatAm trade deadlock; escrow without a bank.
- Kill shot: feature of the rail, not a category; the hard part (event verification/disputes) is NOT Kira's moat.
- Verdict: CUT.

### 4. Compliance-as-code FX
- Steelman: 2026 regulatory tailwind; first-mover sets the default.
- Kill shot: narrow, jurisdictional, backend plumbing; no headline story.
- Verdict: CUT.

### 5. A2A payment accounts
- Steelman: boldest, most non-obvious; best fit for the 10-year/billion metric; agent-native = Kira DNA.
- Kill shot: thin near-term revenue; "nobody builds this" claim is fragile (see Log 2); "billion PEOPLE" metric needs a defended reframe.
- Verdict: SURVIVES — if narrowed to the gap others left open (the account, not the rail). Stress-tested in Log 4.

## Resolution
Winner: **#5, narrowed to "the agent banking layer for LatAm."**
Rejected (named, for the memo's 20% "why this one"):
- CFO-agent — already in production, not non-obvious.
- Self-reconciling — strong moat, no end-user story.
- Conditional payouts — incremental, not Kira's muscle.
- Compliance-as-code — plumbing, no headline.
De-risk: Year-1 beachhead absorbs the useful half of CFO-agent as real revenue.
